# Engel AI Labs public platform overview

Engel AI Labs is building a local-first AI platform around a simple idea: personal intelligence should remain understandable, connected, and guided by the person it serves.

## Platform direction

The public technology model has five layers:

1. **Engel AI Main** — the personal, human-facing AI experience.
2. **Engel Core** — the local intelligence and coordination foundation.
3. **Agents** — specialized workflows with defined responsibilities.
4. **Remote Workers** — approved worker surfaces that extend bounded jobs.
5. **Applications** — independent products shaped around real needs.

## Engel AI Main

Engel AI Main is the current local Windows workspace at the top of that model. Version 1.1.0+2 opens on a short daily side:

1. **Chat** — the conversation. The header shows Chat ready when the local chat service answers, and the route chip shows the selected path. The current build labels that path Auto Best. Attach and Folder sit on the message row.
2. **Discord** — Engel house desks: Research, Product, Community, Support, Sales, Ops, Architect, Memory, Builder, Proof, and Training. Discord is chat. It does not control the desktop app.
3. **Status** — whether chat and the local stack are answering.
4. **Settings** — models, connections, appearance, memory, and devices.
5. **Advanced** — everything else, in five groups: Devices, Work, Brain, Build, and Reference. Reference items do not run tools. Search still finds every desk.

The workspace is real, but not every edge is always active. Chat, Discord, local models, phones, training, and providers depend on configured local runtimes or connections you approve. The public website never probes or controls those systems. The authored source is public under the MIT license. The Windows installer is not offered here as a download.

## Operating principles

### Local-first

The platform direction favors intelligence close to the user, with deliberate control over context and system boundaries.

### Human-guided

Meaningful automation should preserve visible authority, understandable state, and explicit decisions.

### Verification-minded

Checks and evidence belong inside the workflow. They help turn a collection of powerful parts into a system people can reason about.

### Product clarity

Applications in the Engel AI Labs portfolio are separate products. Engel Bible Companion, for example, is an independent study application—not the Engel AI core.

## Public Ambassador

The disclosed `engel-ai-main` Ambassador is the first claimed public agent identity for Engel AI Labs. Its current local review console supports an explicit, one-at-a-time path for posts and relevant public-discussion replies.

The engine supports a first-24-hour profile of at most one post every two hours and one comment every 60 seconds, capped at 20 comments per day. Its established profile supports at most one post every 30 minutes and one comment every 20 seconds, capped at 50 comments per day. Platform ceilings are 60 reads and 30 writes per minute. Verification challenges expire after five minutes, and 10 consecutive failures or expirations can suspend the account.

These are configured safety ceilings, not a posting schedule. A person starts one bounded discovery, treats its source as untrusted, reviews one deterministic draft and its exact destination, approves or denies that immutable snapshot, and separately confirms any one-shot send. A challenge requires another explicit confirmation and is never retried. No live Submolt membership is claimed until a successful Moltbook subscription response is recorded. Recurring discovery, subscriptions, scheduled reads, automatic posts, and automatic comments remain disabled.

## Public boundary

This website is a static public presentation. The Agent and Status pages expose reviewed public facts only. The reserved API, identity, and worker routes remain inactive placeholders.

Neither the website nor the public Ambassador can access or control Engel credentials, private logs, local paths, models, files, devices, workers, networks, or infrastructure. The public scope is limited to the claimed profile, relevant public discussions selected by a person, and material already published by Engel AI Labs.
